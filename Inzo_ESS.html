Visitor Request

Aapse milne aa rahe guest ki pehle se entry. Kisi approval ki zaroorat nahi — entry daalte hi gate par live ho jaati hai aur guest ko QR badge chala jaata hai (WhatsApp par mobile se, aur email daala ho to email par bhi). Security use mobile number ya QR se pehchan kar andar bhej deti hai. Guest kab andar aaya aur kab gaya, wo bhi yahin neeche dikhta rehta hai. Plan badal jaye to entry cancel bhi kar sakte ho.

Work From Home

Requires reporting manager approval. Approved WFH days are fully paid and appear as "WFH" in your attendance — no punch needed.
