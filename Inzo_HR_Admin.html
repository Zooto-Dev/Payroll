Import Employees

How it works: ① Download the template and fill it in Excel → ② paste the rows (with header) below or upload the CSV → ③ Preview → ④ Import. Employee codes are generated automatically from the Unit — you don't enter a code.
Dates: DD/MM/YYYY or YYYY-MM-DD. Unit: must match one of your units (code or name) — the code auto-generates from it (e.g. ZM01 → ZM01044). Gross Salary: the monthly gross; it stays pending CEO approval.
